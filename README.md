# Albert's Last Roundup 🐄

A farewell mini-game for Albert — herd as many cows as you can in 60 seconds!

**▶️ Play it here: https://ivanatso-conveyor.github.io/albert-cow-roundup/**

## How to play

- **Hand tracking (recommended):** Allow camera access, show your hand, and Albert chases it. **Pinch** your thumb and index finger next to a cow to scoop it into the basket on his back.
- **Mouse:** Move to lead Albert, click to grab.
- **Phone/tablet:** Drag your finger to run, tap to grab — works alongside hand tracking too.

## Watch out for

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Golden cow** | Worth 3 cows! |
| 🐺 **The wolf** | Chases Albert — if he catches you, every cow in your basket escapes. Run! |
| 💩 **Poops** | Slip on one and Albert falls flat and drops his basket. Walk back over the basket to pick it up. |
| 🕶️ **Nadim** | Dashes in to chase off the wolf, pet a cow, and wave hi to Albert. |

Scores are saved to a leaderboard in your browser — pass one laptop around at the party for a shared high-score board.
